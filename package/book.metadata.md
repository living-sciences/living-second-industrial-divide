# Source-of-record metadata — Piore & Sabel (1984), The Second Industrial Divide

This corpus entry is a BOOK, not a paper. There is deliberately NO `paper.pdf`:
the work is in copyright (Basic Books, 1984; renewed paperback 1986) and no
lawful machine-readable full text is freely obtainable. Do not fetch the text
from shadow libraries or re-host any scan. See `PROVENANCE.md` and the Phase 0
briefing for what may be used instead.

## Bibliographic record

- Michael J. Piore & Charles F. Sabel, *The Second Industrial Divide:
  Possibilities for Prosperity*. New York: Basic Books, 1984. 355 pp. text
  (listed 368/372 pp. incl. front matter in library records).
- ISBNs: 0-465-07561-4 / 978-0-465-07561-4 (hardcover, 1984);
  0-465-07562-2 / 978-0-465-07562-1 (paperback, 1986); library records also
  carry 0-465-07563-0. Open Library work: OL3508690W (3 editions).
- Still in print via Basic Books / Hachette (publisher page:
  https://www.hbglibrary.com/titles/michael-piore/the-second-industrial-divide/9780465075614/).
- Won a MacArthur Foundation award citation; one of the most-cited works in
  political economy / economic sociology (tens of thousands of citations).

## Lawful access routes to the full text (all require a human or an account)

- **Internet Archive controlled digital lending**: scans exist as
  `secondindustrial0000pior` (1984 ed., 372 images; collections
  `printdisabled`+`internetarchivebooks` — print-disabled access only) and
  `secondindustrial00pior` (collection `inlibrary` — borrowable 1-hour/14-day
  loans with a free IA account). Used here for METADATA AND PAGE-NUMBER
  VERIFICATION ONLY; no scraping of page images or OCR text.
- **Purchase**: in-print paperback and ebook via the publisher; used hardcovers
  are cheap. A lawfully purchased copy for internal (non-redistributed)
  claim-attribution checks is the flagged human gate for later phases.
- **Google Books**: snippet view only (volume lookup rate-limited at fetch
  time, 2026-10-27); usable for verifying exact wording of short quotes.
