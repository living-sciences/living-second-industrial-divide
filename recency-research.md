## 1. The current world, in brief

The reader asks whether globalization became a new mass-production system —
the book's own Exit 1 (prediction P2). Forty-two years of evidence now exist,
and the 2020s added a twist the 1990s-2000s GVC literature did not have: GVC
participation has been flat-to-falling since ~2011 ("slowbalisation"), and
since 2018-2022 policy actively reverses the integration trend (tariff war,
reshoring/friend-shoring, CHIPS/IRA-style industrial policy; WTO GVC
Development Report 2025, staged, measures all of this: US-China bilateral
trade ~30% below counterfactual since 2018; Mexico overtook China as the top
US import source in late 2023). So the question is no longer "is
globalization becoming mass production?" but "WAS the 1990-2015 globalization
wave a mass-production regime, and is what's now decaying Exit 1, Exit 2, or
neither?" — a genuinely answerable 40-year verdict rather than a forecast.

Key present-day facts the studies will sit on (each from a staged source):

- China produced ~35% of world manufacturing OUTPUT and 29% of manufacturing
  VALUE ADDED in 2023 vs the US's 12%/16% (Schwartz 2024, staged; our staged
  WDI extract independently gives CHN = 27.9% of world manufacturing VA 2023 —
  the two agree within revision noise, and the output/VA inversion is itself
  evidence in the H-contest: scale sits in China, profit sits with lead firms).
- World trade in UNCTAD-SoP intermediate goods is a FLAT ~20-24% share of
  exports 1988-2023 (staged WITS extract) — the unbundling signal lives in
  the broader BEC definition and in TiVA value-added measures, a data seam
  every verdict must respect (see §5).
- US manufacturing has gone majority-EAF (minimill-style) while China's crude
  steel is ≈91.6% basic-oxygen (staged worldsteel 2025) — the same industry
  yields a flexible-specialization verdict in the US and a mass-production
  verdict in China. A preview that the dichotomy may fail on geography, not
  on evidence.
- US establishment-size data now run 1978-2023 (staged BDS 2023 release):
  the D1 claim (small-establishment shift) is checkable through the present.
- Smartphone diffusion (staged WDI mobile subs/100 for CHN/IND/VNM/BRA/MEX/
  IDN) gives the "global mass-consumption market" demand-side test P2's
  institutional half implies: developing-world demand materialized, but via
  market-led diffusion, not "multinational Keynesianism".

## 2. Prior art — who already asked this, and what is left to add

Ordered by proximity to the reader's question:

1. **Kaplinsky 2023** (*Papeles de Europa* 36, OA — STAGED at Phase 2a; the
   closest single text). Argues GVCs SUSTAINED the mass-production paradigm
   from the 1980s and reads the post-2008 GVC decline as that paradigm's
   fracture — i.e., an explicit H-A answer, argued at paradigm level with
   aggregate participation trends. He does NOT test it against the book's
   fork, nor against falsifiable per-signature data (plant scale, governance
   mix, variety trends), nor against H-B/H-C alternatives.
2. **Schwartz 2024** (Phenomenal World, staged; the 40th-anniversary
   assessment). Grades the book's hits (vertical disintegration happened;
   mass production's rigidity diagnosis) and misses (five omissions: profit
   conflict among firms/IPR monopoly, dependent-not-egalitarian
   disintegration, Mark-2 innovation, finance, geopolitics), and sketches a
   "third divide". Engages P2 only in passing (the China output/VA numbers).
   No systematic data test of the Exit-1-vs-Exit-2 fork; proposes his own
   third frame instead (close to H-C).
3. **Baldwin 2006 (staged) + The Great Convergence 2016**: the "second
   unbundling" — ICT + wage gaps fragmenting production stage-by-stage — is
   the canonical H-C statement; Baldwin 2011 (NBER WP 17716) argues 20th-c.
   trade theory (and by extension the book's plant-level categories) no
   longer fits task-level trade.
4. **Gereffi, Humphrey & Sturgeon 2005** (staged, INTERNAL ONLY, cite DOI):
   the governance taxonomy (market/modular/relational/captive/hierarchy) is
   the analytic language for H-B — modular/relational GVC governance is
   district logic at global scale — and for H-A's counter-reading (captive +
   hierarchy = mass-production control). GHS themselves note the flexible-
   specialization lineage. They classify; they do not adjudicate the P&S fork.
5. **Sturgeon 2002/2008** (2008 staged, INTERNAL ONLY): "modular production
   networks" — contract manufacturers switching customers/products like
   district firms — an explicit H-B mechanism in the electronics industry.
6. **Milberg & Winkler 2013** (*Outsourcing Economics*, CUP — paywalled,
   record only): GVCs as lead-firm cost-capture and financialization —
   supports the Schwartz/H-C "power asymmetry" reading with trade data.
7. **Hirst & Zeitlin 1991** (paywalled, recorded): already adjudicated
   "flexible specialization vs post-Fordism" conceptually — the warning that
   the categories blur if stated loosely; our pre-declared signatures answer
   exactly that critique.
8. **Herrigel & Zeitlin (2010s)**: disintegrated production without
   convergence — firms mix practices; a standing argument that the dichotomy
   fails at firm level.
9. **Williams et al. 1987** (paywalled; H2 gate): the contemporary
   claims-audit of the book — prior art for study 001, not 002.
10. **2020s empirics**: WTO GVC Development Report 2025 (staged):
    participation plateaued since 2011, reglobalization-with-rerouting;
    Antràs "De-globalisation?" (2020): slowbalisation ≠ deglobalization;
    reshoring literature 2024-25 (friend-shoring evidence stronger than
    reshoring evidence). On the H-B frontier: Shein-style ultra-responsive
    supply chains (100-200-unit test batches, 5-day restock — company-claimed
    numbers; treated as case color, never as a graded data leg) and the
    mass-customization/C2M literature.

**The novelty argument (what 002 adds beyond all of the above).** Kaplinsky
asserts H-A; Schwartz narrates five omissions and gestures at H-C; Baldwin
built H-C; GHS/Sturgeon built H-B's language. NONE of them (a) state the
three readings as rival hypotheses WITH pre-declared falsifiable signatures,
(b) grade the book's own P2 prediction as written against those signatures on
post-1984 data in one place, or (c) allow and operationalize the verdict "the
book's dichotomy itself fails" as a first-class outcome. The contribution is
the assembly + the explicit adjudication contract, exactly the living-paper
form: not a new theory, but the book's fork finally scored, with the
competing readings engaged by name. (R4 discipline: 002's findings must cite
Kaplinsky 2023, Schwartz 2024, Baldwin 2006/2016, GHS 2005, Sturgeon 2008,
Milberg & Winkler 2013, Hirst & Zeitlin 1991 — and claim novelty ONLY in the
terms above.)

## 4. Power / discrimination analysis (why the three hypotheses are separable on this data)

The design treats H-A/H-B/H-C as predictions over SEVEN pre-declared
signatures (spec §3). Separation claims, honestly stated:

- **Strong separation** (different signs predicted): S1 plant/unit scale
  (H-A: rising at the global production unit; H-B: falling/stable); S4
  governance mix (H-A: captive/hierarchy dominant; H-B: modular/relational);
  S6 demand institutions (H-A-as-written requires global demand management —
  checkable as ABSENCE, the cleanest single test in the set: the
  institutional half of P2 verifiably did not happen).
- **Moderate separation**: S2 product variety/batch (H-A: standardization;
  H-B: variety growth — SKU/variety proxies are imperfect, so this signature
  can return "not measurable at scale" and says so); S3 trade composition
  (H-C predicts the intermediates/tasks trend BETTER than either exit; the
  SoP-vs-BEC seam limits level claims to trends).
- **Weak separation, declared**: S5 skill content (all three allow
  polarization stories; graded cautiously, mirror of D2's [LIT] status); S7
  district persistence (H-B-specific: supports H-B if present, but its
  absence also fits H-C).
- **The dichotomy-fails outcome is reachable**: if S1 splits by geography
  (China mega-plants AND Western smallization — the steel preview), S4 mixes
  governance types within one chain, and S3's task-level trend dominates,
  the pre-declared verdict is H-C/"the fork was the wrong axis", and the
  study says the BOOK's own frame cannot score its own 40-year test. That is
  a legitimate, pre-declared finding, not a failure of the study.

Era structure sharpens power: 1984-1995 (pre-GVC), 1995-2011 (deep
globalization — the H-A/H-B contest's home era), 2011-2026 (slowbalisation +
decoupling — where Kaplinsky's "fracture" reading and H-C diverge: if
globalization WAS mass production, its decay should track the paradigm's
fracture; if it was unbundling, decay should track ICT/geopolitics, not
scale economics).

## 6. The H1 dependency (how the blocking gate is structured in the specs)

H1 (lawful book copy) blocks only "the book says X at p.Y" attribution — it
does not block computing today's series. So the specs split every
book-anchored element into a two-branch structure:

- **Provisional branch (runs now):** the claim/prediction is stated in the
  Phase 0 briefing's wording, anchored to ≥2 independent quoting sources
  (named per row: e.g. C1's Emilia-Romagna trajectory anchored to Rinaldi WP
  + ISTAT Storie di dati, NOT to the book's famous "second place" sentence,
  which circulates in incompatible tertiary versions). Verdict rows carry the
  tag `book-anchor: PROVISIONAL-PENDING-H1 (sources: …)`.
- **H1 branch (pre-registered, runs after the gate):** a read-over-text pass
  replaces each provisional anchor with the book's own wording + page cite,
  and every verdict whose GRADE could change under the book's actual wording
  is re-issued; the re-grade diff ships as an appendix. If H1 never clears
  AND a claim cannot be double-sourced, that row is published as
  "not-checkable (attribution)" — never silently dropped, never graded on a
  tertiary quote.

Nothing in 001/002 publishes a page-cited book value before H1. The page
itself (Phase 3) states the provisional status in its methods note (R2).
