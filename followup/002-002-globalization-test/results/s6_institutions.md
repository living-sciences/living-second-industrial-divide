## Result (institutions required: 0 of 2 exist)
The institutional half of Exit 1 is absent: none of the demand-management institutions P2-as-worded required (0 of 2) was constructed; developing-world mass consumption did arrive on market terms - mobile subscriptions per 100 people rose from near zero in 1995 (China 0.3, India 0.0, Viet Nam 0.0) to at or above saturation by 2023 (China 128.2, India 80.6, Viet Nam 131.0), but it was delivered by WTO-era market integration and private GVC participation, not by any 'multinational Keynesian' demand-coordinating institution - so the global mass-consumption market exists while the institutions that P&S's Exit 1 said would stabilize it do not.
